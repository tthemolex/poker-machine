| med | M1 | **`bootloader_random_enable()` is used outside its API contract.** The IDF header (`bootloader_random.h`) says disable "must be called ... before RF features, **ADC**, or I2S are initialized". The firmware initialises and uses the ADC first (`Machine::begin` sets the GPIO34 attenuation, `setup()` reads the battery), then toggles the entropy source on every deal. If the SAR ADC is not fully restored after `bootloader_random_disable()`, `readVbat()` returns wrong values. A reading near full scale (~13.6 V) would silently **disable the low-battery refusal**. FIRMWARE.md admits it is unverified ("check on the hardware"). | `machine.cpp:31-35,404-412`, `config.h:134` | Add a plausibility guard: if Vbat after a deal differs from the last pre-deal reading by > 1 V, or is > 13.0 V, treat it as invalid, warn, and set `HW_ENTROPY_DURING_DEAL` false for the session. Or gather entropy once in `setup()` before any ADC init (enable, collect 256 bits from `esp_random()` into a CSPRNG/SplitMix seed mixed with `esp_random()`, disable). Add a BUILD 8.2 step: compare `vbat` before and after `deal burn`. |
| med | M2 | **D-20 residual risk: a burn card can land face up.** A burn leaves the slot at about Z_EJECT ≈ 122 mm above the table at the lowest PWM, tips over the slot lip and falls ~120 mm past the base wall (same Ø199).
