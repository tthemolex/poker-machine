## Other defects

### HIGH

* **H1 Burn drawer cannot plausibly catch a burn card** (D-15, `burn_tray.scad`, `section_rack_top.png`). When the card tail leaves the nip (Y 65), the card front is at Y 128.5, which is 33 mm outside the shell (inner R ≈ 95.7). Its centre of mass (Y ≈ 96.8) sits right on the slot lip, so it tips out onto the table or back in. Also, the open drop zone between the idler and the shell is about 23-25 mm, but the card is 63.5 mm. The tray's inner X width is 2 x 44.6 = 89.2 mm for an 88.9 mm card (0.15 mm per side), so a tumbling card will wedge. Required: a burn path that works geometrically. For example, a deflector or trap door below the nip so the card is caught before more than half of it has passed the nip, plus a wider tray (≥ 2 mm per side). Or justify it with a real test and log it as a known issue.
* **H2 Loader without a pinch roller (D-14) is not accepted.** The spec requires "a pinch roller into the shelf", and it says "do not trade reliability for size". With the rollers at Y −26, the card loses drive at front Y 37.5 and must coast ≥ 15 mm through a 0.6 mm slot, rubbing on 0.9 mm fins, before it reaches the stop at 52.5. Coast distance depends on speed and friction, and the stop must not bounce. That is a reliability trade made for size. Required: a positive drive until the card is fully in the shelf. For example, move the drive roller to the throat (Y ≈ −16) with a pinch idler built into the gate, cutting the coast to about 5 mm; or add a pinch pair at the rack back face. Re-run cardpath and interference.
* **H3 Turret cable path is blocked by the battery.** The deck cable hole (Ø36, `base_deck.scad`) is centred over the battery. Battery top is at 29.6 and deck underside at 30.0, so there is a **0.4 mm** gap. The bearing ID space (deck to turntable, inside the Ø70 ring) has no radial exit. The harness for 2 steppers, servo, N20, 130 motor, 2 IR pairs, microswitch and hall cannot get from the bearing centre to the ±Y electronics crescents. The service loop described in BUILD.md step 12 therefore has no physical route. Required: a modelled cable channel, e.g. a notch in the deck plus a recess/groove in the cradle or battery, or a raised deck, then re-check height.
* **H4 No harness exits inside the turret.** The turntable cable hole at (0, −24) opens inside the closed `loader_frame` box. No opening is modelled from that box to the rack NEMA (−X), the microswitch (+X rail frame) or the launcher (+Y). Required: wire windows or clips, shown in a render.

### MEDIUM

* M1 Sub-1.2 mm walls around heat-set inserts: rack_nut_arm 0.7 mm, launcher_floor 0.75 mm (see #2/#5).
* M2 `loader_frame` turntable fixings: 2.9 mm pockets for 5.7 mm inserts, 0.1 mm roof.
* M3 `base_shell` door-rebate ring is a 4 mm cantilever at z 1.2, misdocumented as a bridge.
* M4 `rack_motor_mount`: 1.0 mm seat plate; a 47 mm bridge with a Ø24 hole in it.
* M5 `TOL` is not used anywhere (criterion 5).
* M6 `SERVO_SWEEP` is not tied to firmware PUSH_OUT/PUSH_HOME.
* M7 Serviceability: the shell is fixed by CSK screws **from below the turntable** (SHELL_TAB inserts at TT_TOP), so the shell cannot come off without lifting the turret off the base. Jam clearing relies on the lift-off cap (top access to the rack and hopper) and the burn drawer (access to the launcher). The pusher is reachable only after removing the hopper. That is plausible but tight; BUILD.md should say how to clear a jam at the eject station.

* L1 Raw OpenSCAD meshes contain zero-volume slivers that only the export cleanup removes (#1).
* L2 Exterior render names are swapped (front vs back). The README hero is the UI side, not the exit side.
* L3 No render shows the DC charge jack.
* L4 The ALLOWED overlap list has no volume cap.
* L5 Most fasteners, the M8 ballast nuts and the wiring are not modelled.
* L6 Small sub-1.2 features: hall_post groove 0.9, loader_coupler 0.75, hopper_gate IR pocket 0.5, ui_bezel recess 0.8-1.0, base_deck 0.5, turntable magnet skin 0.4, launcher_floor spring-seat sliver 0.1.
